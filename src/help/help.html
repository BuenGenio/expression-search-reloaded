Expression Search Reloaded

Gmail-like search expressions for Thunderbird's quick filter bar.

Quick start

Show the quick filter bar (Ctrl+Shift+K, or the filter button in the message list header) and type into the Expression search box. The current folder is filtered while you type.

  • weekend plans – messages with “weekend plans” in From, To, Cc or Subject. With Act like the normal quick filter enabled (default), input without operators behaves exactly like Thunderbird's own filter, including the Sender / Recipients / Subject / Body buttons.
  • from:fred to:tom a:yes or f:fred t:tom a:yes – from Fred to Tom with an attachment.
  • s:invoice t:(alice -bob) – subject contains “invoice”, sent to Alice but not to Bob.
  • f:(amazon or ebay) older_than:1y – from Amazon or eBay, older than a year.

While you type, a popup under the box explains the operator you are typing and shows how the expression was understood. It closes when you press Enter, leave the box or select a message. Hover over the box to see notes about the last search, such as errors or how many messages could be body-searched. Click ? next to the box to open this page.

Dates like 2024/03/01, 2024-03-01 14:30 are local time; other formats are passed to JavaScript's Date parser (e.g. Mon, 25 Dec 1995 13:30:00 GMT). Thunderbird compares before/after dates by calendar day: after:2024/03/01 finds messages from March 2 on, and af:(2024/03/01 -2024/03/09) finds March 2–9. For times, use a time of day (af:9:00) or date:. Operators marked “rest of the input” take everything that follows them as their value, so they must come last.

Combining

  • Terms next to each other must all match: f:bob s:report. You may write and.
  • or (lower case) means either: f:bob or f:dave.
  • - negates: -f:spam, f:-spam, -(s:a or s:b).
  • Parentheses group, and an operator applies to a whole group: t:(alice or bob), f:(-foo -bar).
  • Words without operators form a phrase: s:electric bill searches for “electric bill”.
  • Double quotes make text literal: s:"this or that".
  • The expression is combined with the other quick filter buttons (Unread, Starred, Tags, …) and with the normal text filter (if shown); all of them must match.

Any mix of and, or and - is evaluated correctly in the message list (it is converted to the form Thunderbird's search engine understands). Extremely large combinations are rejected with a message instead of slowing Thunderbird down.

Calculator: input that looks like arithmetic (3*(4+5)) is calculated when you press Enter.

Keys

Ctrl+Shift+KShow the quick filter bar and focus the expression box (when the normal filter box is hidden, which is the default).
EnterSearch now (without waiting for the typing delay); optionally selects the first result.
Ctrl+EnterCreate or update the “ExpressionSearch” saved search over all folders of the account.
Shift+EnterGlobal search (gloda) for the words of the expression in all indexed folders.
↓Move to the message list.
EscClear the box; press again to clear the other quick filters / close the bar.

Saved search (Ctrl+Enter)

A saved search (virtual folder) shows the result of a search over many folders and is updated every time you open it. Ctrl+Enter creates – or updates – a saved search called ExpressionSearch for the current expression, searching all real folders of the current account. Rename it to keep it; the next Ctrl+Enter then creates a new one.

In the options you can choose the folder in which it is created, keep the folder selection of an existing ExpressionSearch folder (edit its properties once to choose which folders it searches), and open it in a new tab.

Click to search

Hold Ctrl (and, if enabled in the options, Shift) and right-click the subject, sender, recipient, correspondent, tags or date of a message in the message list (table or cards view) to find messages with the same value. The message list context menu also has an Expression Search submenu for the same purpose.

  • Prefixes such as “Re:”, “Fwd:”, “AW:” and “[list-name]” are removed from subjects.
  • Optionally a regular expression rewrites the subject first. Several matches are combined with or. Example: match \[#(\d+)\], replace $1; subject “[#1212][#2323] Some text” searches for (1212 or 2323).
  • Optionally only the part before the “@” of e-mail addresses is used.

Message filters, search dialog and saved searches

The add-on also adds these search criteria to Thunderbird's filter editor, the Search Messages dialog and saved search properties: Bcc, To Somebody Only, Subject (RegEx), Subject (Long), Header (RegEx), From (RegEx), Recipients (To/Cc/Bcc RegEx), Time (Every Day), Date Match, Attachment Name/Type, Body (RegEx). Filters that use them need the add-on to be enabled.

Limitations

  • Body, attachment name and body regex searches can only read messages that are stored locally (Local Folders, or IMAP folders synchronised for offline use). The help popup tells you how many messages could be searched. Regular expressions are run by Thunderbird itself; a pathological pattern on a huge folder can make Thunderbird slow until it finishes.
  • headerre only sees headers that Thunderbird stores in its message database. Add them in the options (Searchable headers); existing messages are only included after Repair Folder.
  • Saved searches store their terms in Thunderbird's own format, which cannot express parentheses and is evaluated from left to right. One or group combined with other terms is stored exactly; with two or more or groups (e.g. f:(a or b) s:(c or d)) the saved search may show more messages than the message list, and the help popup says so.
  • After disabling or updating the add-on, criteria used in message filters keep their old behaviour until Thunderbird is restarted (Thunderbird does not allow removing them earlier).

Privacy

Everything happens inside Thunderbird. The add-on makes no network connections, collects no data and only stores its settings locally. With Log searches to the Error Console enabled, your search expressions are written to Thunderbird's console (which stays on your computer).

What changed in version 5 (compared with 2.4beta)

  • Rewritten for Thunderbird 153 and later (new mail tab, quick filter bar, ES modules, Manifest V3).
  • Fixed: negated attachment-name, date and From/To regex searches; y (years) unit; tags that match nothing; Ctrl+Enter and Shift+Enter; calculator negation; mixed and/or expressions; header names in headerre.
  • Removed options that only applied to the old quick filter bar (moving the box to other toolbars, button label and result label sizes) and the status bar icon; help and options are reachable from the ? button and the Add-ons Manager.
  • No more help/donation window at every start.
